# Patient Orbit

A design philosophy for the images in this repo. `scripts/readme-art.py` draws them.

**Form.** Everything returns. The primary figure is the closed curve: a ring drawn once, with a single unbroken stroke, that comes back to where it began. Straight lines exist only to carry one thing to the next, and they turn at right angles when they must turn. Corners are softened just enough to be touched. Every form is meticulously crafted and placed by measurement, never by eye alone.

**Space.** The ground is paper, warm and unhurried, and most of it is left alone. A figure earns its place by what it holds apart. Margins are generous and exact. The quiet around a mark is part of the mark, and the composition reads as the product of deep expertise because nothing in it had to be moved twice.

**Color.** One accent, used the way a signature is used. A warm fired-clay red marks only the points where a person acts, and nowhere else. Everything a machine does stays in ink, in a soft grey rule, or on white. The restraint is the message: when the accent appears, the eye already knows what it means. This calibration is painstaking, and a second accent would undo it.

**Rhythm.** Meaning accumulates through patient repetition. Fine ticks ring the outer edge like the dial of an instrument. Small dots travel the inner rings at intervals that almost repeat and then skip, the way a count is kept by hand. The density rewards a second look and never asks for the first. It should feel like hours of labor by someone at the top of their field, laid down one mark at a time.

**Type.** Words are rare and each has a job. A tall, calm serif names the thing once, at scale. A monospace face whispers the labels in capitals, because they are readings, not sentences. A plain sans carries the one line of explanation that the forms cannot. No word is decoration, no label touches another, and nothing leaves the page.

**Hierarchy.** One thing is large, one thing is colored, and the rest is quiet. A reader should know where to look before knowing what the image is about. Master-level execution here means the hierarchy survives being seen at half size, in passing, on a bright or a dark page.
